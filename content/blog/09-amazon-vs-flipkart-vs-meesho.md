"Which is better — Amazon, Flipkart or Meesho?" is one of the most common shopping questions in India. The honest answer: **it depends on the listing**. Each platform hosts many sellers, and the same product can be a great buy on one store today and on another next week.

Instead of picking a favourite platform, use this framework to compare the **specific listings** in front of you.

## Quick comparison framework

| What to compare | Why it matters | Where to look |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Product & variant | Different variants aren't comparable | Title, specs, "what's in the box" |
| Seller | Affects quality, warranty and returns | "Sold by" section, seller ratings |
| Final price | Fees and offers change the real cost | Checkout summary |
| Delivery | Speed varies by pin code and seller | Delivery estimate on the listing |
| Returns | Policies vary by category and seller | Return/replacement section |
| Payment options | COD, UPI, cards and EMI availability vary | Checkout page |
| Warranty | Brand vs seller warranty | Product page, brand website |

## 1. Product categories and range

All three stores sell across many categories, but their **range and typical sellers differ**:

- Amazon and Flipkart both carry wide selections across electronics, appliances, fashion, home and more, from brands and third-party sellers.
- Meesho is known for affordable fashion, home and lifestyle products, many from small and independent sellers.

What matters is whether the **exact product you want** is available — and from whom.

## 2. Seller information

On every marketplace, look at **who is selling**:

- seller name and **ratings**,
- whether the seller is the **brand or an authorised seller** (important for electronics),
- how long the seller has been active, if shown.

## 3. Pricing — compare the total

Compare the **final checkout amount** for the same variant, including:

- delivery charges,
- cash-on-delivery or platform fees, where applicable,
- instant discounts you definitely qualify for.

Treat conditional bank offers as a bonus. See [how to compare prices across stores](/blog/compare-product-prices-amazon-flipkart-meesho).

## 4. Delivery

Delivery estimates depend on **your pin code, the seller's location and stock**. Some stores offer faster delivery in certain cities or through paid memberships. Check the estimate on each listing with your address.

## 5. Returns and replacements

Return terms differ by **store, category and seller**:

- refund vs replacement vs exchange,
- the number of days allowed,
- conditions such as unused items with tags.

For clothing and footwear, check **size charts** and exchange options carefully.

## 6. Payment options

Most stores support UPI, cards and net banking; **cash on delivery** availability can vary by product, value and location. Always pay inside the official app or website.

## 7. Warranty

For electronics and appliances:

- Is it a **brand warranty**?
- Will you receive a **GST invoice**?
- Does the brand have **service centres** near you?

## How to evaluate a listing in five minutes

1. Confirm **model and variant**.
2. Note the **seller** and their rating.
3. Check the **return policy** for that listing.
4. Compare the **checkout total** across stores.
5. Check the **delivery date** and **warranty**.

Then choose the listing that offers the best **overall** value — not just the lowest number.
