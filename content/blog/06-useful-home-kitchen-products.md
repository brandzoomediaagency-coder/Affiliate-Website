The best home and kitchen purchases aren't always the flashiest. They're the things you reach for every day — and that quietly save you time, space or effort. This guide covers genuinely useful categories and **what to check before buying** each one.

## Kitchen essentials that earn their place

### Knives, peelers and chopping boards
A sharp, comfortable knife makes cooking safer and faster. Look for:
- a **comfortable handle** and good balance,
- **stainless steel** blades that are easy to maintain,
- a chopping board that's **easy to clean** and doesn't slip.

### Measuring and prep tools
Measuring cups and spoons, a sturdy grater and a good set of mixing bowls reduce guesswork, especially for baking.

### Pressure cookers and cookware
- Choose the **capacity** for your household size.
- Check the **base type** (induction-compatible or not).
- Pressure cookers sold in India must meet **BIS certification requirements** — look for the ISI mark.

## Storage solutions

Good storage keeps food fresh and kitchens tidy.

| Material | Strengths | Watch out for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Glass | Doesn't stain or absorb smells, microwave-friendly (if marked) | Heavier, can break |
| Stainless steel | Durable, good for dry goods | Not microwave-safe, can't see contents |
| Food-grade plastic | Light, affordable, stackable | Quality varies; avoid heating unless marked safe |

Tips:
- **Airtight lids** with seals keep staples like atta, rice and snacks fresh.
- **Stackable, uniform shapes** make better use of shelves.
- **Measure your shelves** before buying.

## Cleaning tools

- **Microfibre cloths** clean effectively with less detergent.
- A **good mop and bucket** (spin mops are popular) saves effort — check spare refill availability.
- **Sink organisers** and drying racks keep counters clear.

## Home organisation

Small organisers make a big difference:
- **Drawer dividers** and **under-sink racks**.
- **Over-the-door hooks** and **wall-mounted shelves** for small homes.
- **Vacuum storage bags** for seasonal clothes.

Always check **dimensions and load capacity** — a rack that sags or doesn't fit is wasted money.

## Small appliances: what to check

For mixer grinders, kettles, induction cooktops and similar appliances:

1. **Power (watts)** suited to the job.
2. **Capacity** for your household.
3. **Safety features** such as auto shut-off or overload protection.
4. **Warranty**, including longer coverage on motors where offered.
5. **Service network** in your city.
6. **BEE star label** for covered appliances such as refrigerators and air conditioners.

## Buying checklist

- [ ] Solves a problem you actually have
- [ ] Right size — measured your space
- [ ] Food-safe materials where food is involved
- [ ] Required certification marks present
- [ ] Easy to clean and maintain
- [ ] Warranty and seller checked
- [ ] Return policy understood
