A good budget gadget earns its place by solving a small, repeated annoyance — a dying phone on the commute, a messy desk, a lamp you have to get up to switch off. This guide covers gadget categories that tend to be **genuinely useful at low prices**, and how to avoid the gimmicks.

## The "will I use it weekly?" test

Before buying any gadget, ask:

1. **What problem does it solve?** Be specific.
2. **How often will I use it?** Weekly use is a good bar.
3. **Is it compatible** with my phone, laptop, router or home?
4. **Is it safe and certified**, especially if it involves batteries or mains power?
5. **What do recent, detailed reviews say** about durability?

If you can't answer the first two clearly, it's probably a gimmick.

## Useful gadget categories

### Power banks
Handy for travel and long days. Check:
- **Capacity** (mAh) — remember the usable output is lower than the cell capacity.
- **Output** (watts) and supported fast-charging standards.
- **Safety certification** — battery products should come from reputable brands and carry the BIS Standard Mark with a registration number.

### Chargers and cables
A good charger and cable can noticeably speed up charging:
- Match the **charging standard** your device supports (such as USB Power Delivery).
- Use a **cable rated** for the wattage.
- Prefer **USB-C** for newer devices.

### Smart plugs
Turn ordinary appliances into scheduled or app-controlled ones (great for lamps and water heaters, within the plug's rated load). Check:
- **Load rating** (amps/watts) versus your appliance.
- **Wi-Fi band** (many need 2.4GHz) and app/voice-assistant compatibility.

### Phone and laptop stands
Inexpensive and genuinely helpful for video calls, recipes, study and posture. Look for **stability, adjustability** and **non-slip pads**.

### Rechargeable LED lamps
Useful during power cuts and for reading. Check **brightness levels, battery life claims** in reviews and **USB-C charging**.

### USB hubs and adapters
Essential for thin laptops with few ports. Confirm the **ports you need** (USB-A, HDMI, card reader) and whether it supports **pass-through charging**.

### Bluetooth trackers
Helpful for keys and bags, but **compatibility matters** — some trackers work only with specific phone ecosystems.

## Compatibility checklist

| Gadget | Check compatibility with |
| --- | --- |
| Charger / power bank | Your phone's fast-charging standard and port |
| Smart plug | Router band, app, voice assistant, appliance load |
| USB hub | Laptop port type and display output needs |
| Tracker | Your phone's operating system |
| Stand | Device size and weight |

## Spotting gimmicks

- Vague claims ("military-grade", "10x faster") without specifics.
- Features that duplicate something your phone already does well.
- Very low prices for products that handle **batteries or mains electricity**.
- Reviews that are all short and posted around the same time.
