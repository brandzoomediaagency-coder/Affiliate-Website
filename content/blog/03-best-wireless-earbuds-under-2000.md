Budget wireless earbuds have improved enormously. Under ₹2,000 you can now find earbuds with decent sound, app support and water resistance — but you'll also find plenty of exaggerated claims. This guide explains **what matters at this price** and how to compare models.

## What to expect under ₹2,000

At this budget, realistic expectations are:

- **Good everyday sound** for music, videos and podcasts, often with boosted bass.
- **Basic to decent call quality** — microphones vary a lot between models.
- **A full day of use** combining the earbuds and charging case.
- **Splash resistance** (commonly IPX4 or IPX5).
- **Touch controls** and sometimes a companion app with an equaliser.

Features that are often weaker at this price: active noise cancellation (ANC), microphone noise reduction in busy streets, and advanced codecs.

## Sound quality

- **Tuning matters more than driver size.** A bigger driver number doesn't guarantee better sound.
- **An EQ app** lets you reduce boomy bass or brighten vocals — very useful at this price.
- **Codecs:** SBC is universal; AAC is good with iPhones. Fit has a bigger effect on sound than codec choice.

## Microphone and calls

If you take a lot of calls, prioritise microphones:

- Look for **reviews with call recordings**, especially outdoors.
- "ENC" (environmental noise cancellation) claims vary in effectiveness.

## Battery life and charging

- Brands usually quote **earbud battery** and **total with case** separately. Total figures assume multiple recharges from the case.
- Figures are typically measured at **moderate volume with ANC off**. Expect less in real use.
- Prefer a **USB-C** port. Fast-charging claims (for example "10 minutes for X hours") are convenient if accurate.

## Latency (for gaming and videos)

Bluetooth adds a small delay between video and sound. Many budget earbuds offer a **"gaming" or "low-latency" mode**. It helps, but for competitive gaming wired earphones still have the edge. Check reviews that measure lip-sync and latency.

## Comfort and fit

Fit is the most underrated factor:

- Check that **multiple ear-tip sizes** are included.
- Stem-style and bud-style earbuds fit ears differently — consider your comfort over longer sessions.
- A good seal improves bass and blocks outside noise.

## Water and sweat resistance

IP ratings follow the **IEC 60529** standard:

| Rating | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| IPX4 | Protected against splashes from any direction |
| IPX5 | Protected against low-pressure water jets |
| IPX7 | Protected against temporary immersion (up to 1m) |

The "X" means dust resistance wasn't rated. Even rated earbuds can be damaged if you charge them while wet.

## Connectivity

- **Bluetooth version** (5.x) improves efficiency and stability.
- **Multipoint** (connecting to two devices at once) is handy but uncommon at this price.
- Check **app support** for your phone's operating system.

## How to compare earbuds side by side

| Factor | What to look for |
| --- | --- |
| Fit | Several ear-tip sizes; comfortable for 1–2 hours |
| Sound | Balanced tuning or a usable EQ app |
| Calls | Clear voice in reviews with outdoor tests |
| Battery | Independent tests, not just box claims |
| Charging | USB-C; case size fits your pocket |
| Durability | IPX4 or better; build quality in reviews |
| Warranty | Duration and service process in India |
